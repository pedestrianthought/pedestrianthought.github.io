Outside the walled Old Town, in the industrial area you can find Telliskivi Creative City, containing creative businesses, cafes and galleries. Street art decorates the walls of the buildings: "Dance of Death with Endel" by Edward von Lõngus, "Mundra" by Ron Martin and "Squirrel" by Bordalo II.
