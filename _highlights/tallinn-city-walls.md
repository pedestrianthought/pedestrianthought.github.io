Tallinn's Old Town (Vanalinn) is surrounded by Medieval defensive walls, with gates and towers amidst the fortification. Take a walk around the outside of the walls or a tower walk along the top to view the red roofed buildings of Old Town. Walk through Medieval streets such as St Catherine's passage (Katariina Käik) where stone walls and tombstones demonstrate the history.
